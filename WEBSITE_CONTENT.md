## Core Value Propositions

### Spaces that scale with your thinking
Organize conversations into separate workspaces. Each space becomes a dedicated environment for projects, topics, or teams—keeping your AI interactions structured without sacrificing flexibility.

### Branching powered by curiosity
Every conversation can fork. Explore alternative approaches, test different prompts, or experiment with model responses—all without losing your original thread.

### Models that work for you
Switch between Claude's Sonnet 4.5, Opus 4.1, and Haiku 3.5, or GPT-5, GPT-4o, and o1-preview. Different models for different problems, all in one interface.

---

## Feature Highlights

### Multi-space architecture
Create unlimited spaces to separate work, research, brainstorming, and personal projects. Drag and drop chats to reorder them. Your organization, your rules.

### AI agents with reach
Built-in web search and scraping capabilities. Agents that can gather information, visit pages, and use tools to answer complex questions with real-world data.

### Conversations designed for depth
Full markdown support, syntax-highlighted code blocks, GitHub-flavored tables, and real-time streaming responses. Everything you need to work with AI at a technical level.

### Share what matters
Generate public snapshot links for any chat. No authentication required—anyone with the link can view the frozen conversation. Perfect for collaboration or showcasing results.

---

## Technical Foundation

### Distributed by design
Built on Cloudflare Durable Objects with SQLite persistence. WebSocket connections for real-time streaming. React frontend with Valtio state management.

### Secure and isolated
Google OAuth authentication with per-user isolation. Session management that keeps your spaces private while making sharing effortless when you choose to.
